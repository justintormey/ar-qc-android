AR Builder — QR stickers (3 brackets × 2 faces)

Six stickers covering the three assembly steps. Brackets A, B, C each get two QRs — one on each opposing face. The P sticker goes on the face exposed when the bracket is in its correct orientation for that step; the F sticker goes on the opposite face (the one exposed when the bracket is flipped 180°). Step 1 checks A's orientation (AP/AF), step 2 checks B's (BP/BF), step 3 checks C's (CP/CF). Bracket D has no QR — its correctness is read indirectly via which face of C remains visible after D is attached. Step 3's PASS requires both BP and CP visible in the same frame — see docs/BUILDER-PROP-KIT.md for the full procedure.
